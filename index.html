Questions

Frequently asked

What areas does Fields of Heshbon serve?

Santa Fe, Pojoaque, Tesuque, Nambé, Eldorado and Arroyo Hondo (south of Santa Fe, off Old Las Vegas Highway).

What services do you offer?

Sprinkler and drip repair, irrigation installation, sprinkler blowouts, rainwater catchment, land restoration, erosion control and drainage, French drains, native seeding, permaculture design, landscape design and installation, garden installation and maintenance, garden boxes, and handyman and carpentry work.

Who does the work?

Owner Bradley Babb, an irrigation specialist, permaculturist and carpenter with over two decades of experience in Santa Fe and Northern New Mexico.

Can you work with wells, acequias or rainwater?

Yes. Irrigation is designed around your water source, whether that is city or district water, a private well, acequia water, rainwater catchment, or a mix.
