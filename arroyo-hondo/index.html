Land care & irrigation in Arroyo Hondo

About six miles south of downtown Santa Fe off Old Las Vegas Highway, Arroyo Hondo is known for big lots, open meadows and horse properties at close to 7,000 feet. Large properties need irrigation and drainage planned for the whole site.

What's different about Arroyo Hondo

📘 Read the guide: Getting your yard ready for monsoon season →

Acreage, meadows and horse property

Many Arroyo Hondo homes sit on multi-acre lots with pasture and open meadow. We plan drip zones around the house and gardens, native seeding for open ground, and water-smart layouts that don't try to irrigate everything.

Living next to an arroyo

The neighborhood is named for the Arroyo Hondo, a stream that runs only part of the year and drains toward Cienega Creek. Monsoon storms can send fast water across driveways and slopes, so French drains, swales and erosion control protect homes and roads.

Wells and small water systems

Many properties here use private wells or small community water systems. Efficient drip, leak repair and rooftop catchment take pressure off your supply.

Arroyo Hondo FAQ

Do you serve Arroyo Hondo south of Santa Fe?

Yes. That's the Arroyo Hondo off Old Las Vegas Highway, near Seton Village and Sunlit Hills.

Can you fix runoff and erosion on my property?

Yes. We build French drains, swales, berms and erosion control to slow and redirect monsoon runoff.

Can you set up catchment off a barn or large roof?

Yes. We size cisterns and filters to the roof and connect them to drip for gardens, trees or animals' areas.
