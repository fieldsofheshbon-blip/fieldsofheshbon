Questions

Garden Installation & Maintenance FAQ

What grows well in Santa Fe gardens?

Many vegetables, herbs, fruit trees and native perennials do well with good soil, mulch and drip. Timing around late spring and early fall frosts matters.

Do you offer regular garden maintenance?

Yes. We offer one-time cleanups, seasonal visits or recurring maintenance.

Can you fix my desert soil for growing food?

Yes. Adding compost and organic matter, plus mulching, greatly improves our typically sandy or clay soils.

Do you take care of orchards?

Yes. We do fruit tree planting, pruning, mulching and drip for orchards.
