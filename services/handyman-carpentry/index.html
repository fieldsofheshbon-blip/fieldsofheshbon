Handyman & Carpentry FAQ

What kinds of handyman jobs do you take?

Outdoor carpentry, fences, gates, garden structures and general repairs around the home and property. Ask about your project.

Yes. We build trellises, arbors, pergolas and shade structures sized to your space.

Yes. We repair posts, rails and pickets and re-hang sagging gates.
