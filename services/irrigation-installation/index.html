Questions

Irrigation Installation FAQ

Can you install irrigation that runs off a well?

Yes. We size the system to your well's flow and pressure so the pump doesn't short-cycle, and we lean on drip to stretch your water.

Do I need a backflow preventer?

Yes. A backflow device keeps irrigation water from flowing back into your drinking water, and it's typically required by code. We include it.

Should I put in drip or sprinklers?

Drip for trees, shrubs, beds and gardens. Spray only where you truly need it, like a small lawn. Most high-desert yards end up mostly drip.

Can a new system connect to rainwater catchment?

Yes. Drip zones can run off a cistern with a pump and filter.
