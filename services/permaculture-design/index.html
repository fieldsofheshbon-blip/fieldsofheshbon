Questions

Permaculture Design FAQ

What is permaculture design?

An approach to designing land so it is productive and self-sustaining, using natural patterns of water, soil, plants and sun. The result needs fewer inputs over time.

Does permaculture work in the high desert?

Yes, and it's especially valuable here. Harvesting rain, building soil and choosing adapted plants make the most of limited water.

Do I have to install everything at once?

No. We create a phased plan so you can build in stages as time and budget allow.

Can you install the design too?

Yes. We do the earthworks, catchment, irrigation, beds and planting.
