Irrigation repair and installs, land and drainage work, garden design and care, and carpentry, across Santa Fe, Pojoaque, Tesuque, Nambé, Eldorado and Arroyo Hondo.

Irrigation & Water

  • Sprinkler RepairA soggy spot, a dead strip of lawn, or a zone that won't shut off usually has one cause we can track down.
  • Drip Irrigation RepairDrip is the most efficient way to water in the high desert, but only when it's working.
  • Irrigation InstallationWhether you're on city water, a district, your own well, an acequia, or rainwater, a new system should be designed around that source.
  • Sprinkler BlowoutsWater left in irrigation lines expands when it freezes, cracking pipes, valves and backflow devices.
  • Rainwater CatchmentEvery roof is a water source.

Land & Drainage

  • Land RestorationBare, compacted or eroding ground loses water and soil every time it rains.
  • Erosion Control & DrainageSummer storms can drop a lot of water fast, carving ruts in driveways, washing out slopes and pushing water toward your house.
  • French DrainsWhen water collects against a wall, soaks a low spot, or seeps where it shouldn't, a French drain gives it somewhere to go.
  • Native SeedingNative grasses and wildflowers hold soil, feed pollinators and need little or no water once established.

Gardens & Design

  • Permaculture DesignPermaculture means designing a property so water, sun, soil, plants and people support each other.
  • Landscape Design & InstallationA great Northern New Mexico landscape looks at home in the high desert.
  • Garden Installation & MaintenanceFrom a new kitchen garden to an established orchard, we build gardens that thrive in our soil and climate, then keep them healthy through the season with regular care.
