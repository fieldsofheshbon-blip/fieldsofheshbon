Land & Drainage

Land restoration for tired ground

Bare, compacted or eroding ground loses water and soil every time it rains. We help land heal by slowing water down, holding it on site, and getting native plants established so the ground can rebuild itself.

Questions

Land Restoration FAQ

What kind of land do you restore?

Construction-disturbed lots, overgrazed pasture, eroding slopes, bare yards and ground recovering from drought.

How long does restoration take?

Earthwork shows results with the first good rain. Native plants take one to a few growing seasons to fully establish, depending on moisture.

Do you use native plants?

Yes. Native and well-adapted plants handle our soil, sun and dry spells with little or no irrigation once established.

Can restoration help with erosion?

Yes. Slowing and spreading water is at the heart of restoration, and it directly reduces erosion.
