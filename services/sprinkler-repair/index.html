Sprinkler Repair FAQ

How much does sprinkler repair cost?

Service work and repairs are $125 for the first hour. Estimates are free, and irrigation audits start at $165.

Why won't one of my sprinkler zones turn off?

It's usually a valve stuck open from debris, a worn diaphragm or a bad solenoid. Shut off the system's main valve to stop the water, then call. It's typically a straightforward repair.

Why is part of my lawn dry even though the sprinklers run?

Common causes are clogged or wrong nozzles, tilted or sunken heads, low pressure, or heads spaced too far apart. A coverage check finds which one it is.

Is a wet spot that never dries a leak?

Very often, yes. It can be a cracked line, a leaking fitting, or a valve that seeps. We locate it and repair just that section.

Can you work on any brand of sprinkler system?

Yes. We repair all common brands of heads, valves and controllers.
