Questions

French Drains FAQ

What is a French drain?

A trench filled with gravel and a perforated pipe, usually wrapped in filter fabric. It collects water and carries it underground to a safe place.

Where does the water go?

To a point lower on your property, such as a planted basin, swale or dry well. Never onto a neighbor's property or back toward the house.

Will a French drain fix water near my foundation?

It's often part of the fix, along with grading the soil away from the house and extending downspouts.

How long does a French drain last?

With filter fabric and the right slope, a well-built French drain can work for many years.
