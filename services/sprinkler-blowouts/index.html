Questions

Sprinkler Blowouts FAQ

How much does a sprinkler blowout cost?

Sprinkler blowouts start at $125. Bradley confirms the price for your system before he starts, and estimates are free.

When should I blow out my sprinklers?

In fall before overnight hard freezes, typically October in the Santa Fe area. Earlier is safer at higher elevations.

Can I just drain the system instead?

Draining helps, but low spots and valves often hold water. A blowout with compressed air clears what draining leaves behind.

Do drip systems need a blowout?

Yes. Drip lines, filters and valves can crack too. We clear drip at low pressure so tubing and emitters aren't damaged.

Can you start my system back up in spring?

Yes. Spring start-up includes turning water on slowly, checking every zone and fixing anything winter broke.
