Local know-how

What's different about Pojoaque

A valley of many water sources

The Aamodt case, filed in 1966, settled water rights across the Pojoaque basin. Its regional water system will bring Rio Grande water to about 10,000 people, and hooking up is optional, so some neighbors are on wells and some on the new system. We set up irrigation for whatever you have.

Acequias and presas

Roughly 18 acequias run in the Pojoaque Valley Irrigation District, fed through presas (diversions) off the rivers. We connect gardens to ditch water efficiently and add drip for the days between turns.

A longer growing season

Lower elevation means a longer season for gardens and orchards, and more months of watering. Good drip layouts and timers keep plants healthy without wasting water.

Pojoaque FAQ

Can you set up irrigation for a well, regional water or an acequia?

Yes. We size and design the system for your source, whether that's a domestic well, a regional water connection, ditch water, or a mix.

Do you do sprinkler blowouts in Pojoaque?

Yes. Fall blowouts clear water out of the lines before hard freezes. Book in early fall.

Can you build raised garden beds with drip?

Yes. We build garden boxes and plumb them with drip on a timer.
