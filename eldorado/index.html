What's different about Eldorado

District water or your own well

The Eldorado Area Water & Sanitation District serves almost 3,000 accounts, and many other homes in the area run on private domestic wells. On a well, every leak means extra pump run time and lost groundwater, so we hunt down leaks, regulate pressure and use low-flow drip.

Roofs are a free water source

With water this valuable, rooftop catchment is one of the best investments an Eldorado property can make. We set up gutters, cisterns, filters and pumps that feed drip lines, cutting what you draw from the well or the district.

Open lots need erosion control

Wide-open lots and native grassland can wash out and blow out when they're disturbed. Native seeding, erosion control and small water-harvesting earthworks help stabilize bare ground after construction or drought.

Questions

Eldorado FAQ

Do you work on homes with private wells in Eldorado?

Yes. We work on well-fed and district-fed systems alike. On wells we focus on finding leaks and keeping drip efficient so your pump runs less.

Can rainwater run my drip system?

Yes. A cistern with a filter, and a pump if needed, can supply drip lines for gardens and trees. We size it to your roof and your plants.

Can you help re-seed a bare or disturbed lot?

Yes. We do native seeding along with erosion control, so the seed stays put until it takes hold.

When should I winterize my Eldorado irrigation?

Plan your blowout for October, before hard freezes, and drain any above-ground catchment lines and backflow devices at the same visit.
