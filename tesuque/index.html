What's different about Tesuque

Acequia water runs on the river's schedule

Acequias along the Rio Tesuque, like the Acequia del Rio, still carry water to fields and orchards. Under the Aamodt settlement rules, upstream acequias have to stop diverting when the creek runs very low, so water reaches the Pueblo of Tesuque. That's why a backup source matters.

Backup water for dry spells

When the ditch is off, gardens and young trees still need water. We add drip fed by a well or a rain cistern so plantings get through a dry spring or a late monsoon.

Hillsides above the valley

Homes on the slopes above the creek deal with runoff, washouts and steep beds. Terracing, French drains and erosion control keep soil in place and water moving the right direction.

Tesuque FAQ

Can you work with my acequia water?

Yes. We design gardens and orchards that use your ditch water, and we work around your turnouts and the acequia's rules. Your mayordomo and commission set the schedule, and we plan around it.

What can I do when the ditch runs dry in summer?

Add a backup. Drip fed by a well or a rainwater cistern keeps gardens and young trees alive until ditch water is back.

Do you handle slope and runoff problems in Tesuque?

Yes. We build erosion control, terraces and French drains for hillside lots.
